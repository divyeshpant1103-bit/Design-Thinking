About GEU Nexus 🎓

GEU Nexus is an all-in-one student platform designed to connect the Graphic Era University campus community, making campus life, housing, and food discovery easier.

🚀 Developed as part of the Design Thinking Project
Problem Addressed: Fragmentation of campus information, lost items, and finding local food/amenities.
Tech Stack: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6), LocalStorage API, Google Sheets API.
